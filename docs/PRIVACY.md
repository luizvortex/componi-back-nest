# Privacy Policy

**Effective:** 2026-04-23
**Version:** `2026-04-23`

This policy explains how **Componi** ("we", "the service") handles your data. We operate under the Brazilian *Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados* (**LGPD**, Lei nº 13.709/2018) and align with the **EU GDPR** where it applies. If any provision is found unenforceable in your jurisdiction, the rest still stands.

## 1. Who is the controller

Componi is the **data controller** (*controlador* — LGPD Art. 5º VI) for the data described below. You can reach the privacy contact at **privacy@componi.dev**.

## 2. What we collect

**Account data (you give us):**

- Email address, username, display name, avatar URL, optional bio and website URL.
- OAuth provider identifier (GitHub or Google) — we never see or store your OAuth password.

**Content (you publish):**

- Components you create: name, description, source code, dependencies, tags, thumbnail.
- Comments, likes, favorites, follows, collections, shares.
- Reports you file against other users' content.

**Usage metadata (the platform generates):**

- Request IP address and user-agent string, used for rate-limiting and abuse detection and attached to privileged actions in the audit log.
- Approximate timestamps of your actions (create/edit/delete events).

**Moderation data (we generate when applicable):**

- If your account is reported, suspended, or warned, we keep a record of the reason, the acting moderator, and the outcome.

**Derived data (computed from your content):**

- **Component embeddings** — a 384-dimensional numeric vector summarising the public components you publish, used solely to power semantic search. The vector is generated by a local model running in our process; **your content is never sent to an external AI service**. Embeddings are not personal data on their own (they're noise without the original text alongside) and they're regenerated automatically when the corresponding component is updated, deleted, or anonymized.
- **Realtime presence** — when you keep a browser tab open with a live connection, we hold the mapping `userId → socket id` in process memory so we can push notifications. This state lives only as long as the connection; it is never written to disk or shared.

We do **not** collect: tracking cookies, analytics beacons, biometric identifiers, payment information (the service is free), precise geolocation.

## 3. Why we collect it — legal bases (LGPD Art. 7º)

| Data | Legal basis |
|------|------------|
| Email, OAuth identity | Execution of contract (Art. 7º V) — you can't log in without it |
| Published content | Consent (Art. 7º I) — you choose to publish |
| IP / user-agent on privileged actions | Legitimate interest (Art. 7º IX) — abuse prevention & audit |
| Moderation records | Regular exercise of rights (Art. 7º VI) — we need evidence for appeals |
| Audit logs | Legal obligation (Art. 7º II) — LGPD Art. 37 requires operation records |

## 4. Who we share data with

- **Supabase** (auth + database host, based in the US with EU datacenters). Subject to a Data Processing Agreement. See <https://supabase.com/privacy>.
- **GitHub / Google** as OAuth identity providers — only the identifier and email you consent to share are sent back to us.
- **Law enforcement** only when compelled by valid legal process, and we publish transparency reports once a year.

We do **not** sell, rent, or barter your data. We do not use your content to train ML models.

## 5. How long we keep it

| Category | Retention |
|----------|-----------|
| Active account data | For as long as the account exists |
| Published components / comments | Retained after account deletion (anonymized — see §6) because other users may have forked or replied |
| Audit logs of privileged actions | 5 years (LGPD Art. 16 V — legitimate interest in defending against legal claims) |
| IP addresses in throttler storage | 24 hours (Redis TTL) |
| Session cache | 30 seconds |
| Soft-deleted content | 30 days, then purged by a scheduled job |
| Component embeddings | Lifecycle of the component; deleted/regenerated when the component is deleted or updated |
| Realtime socket state | Only while your browser tab is connected; never persisted |

## 6. Your rights (LGPD Art. 18)

You can, at any time:

1. **Access** a copy of your data — `GET /users/me/export` returns a JSON dump of everything we hold.
2. **Correct** inaccurate data — `PATCH /users/me`.
3. **Anonymize or delete** your account — `DELETE /users/me`. We clear all PII (email, username, avatar, bio, links), purge private interactions (likes, follows, notifications, collections), and set an irreversible anonymization flag. Public contributions (components, comments you authored) are retained with an anonymized authorship because other users may depend on them (LGPD Art. 16 III).
4. **Port** your data — the `/export` endpoint returns a machine-readable JSON, and it is yours to re-upload anywhere.
5. **Revoke consent** — by deleting your account or stopping use; note that some data (audit logs) is retained on a different legal basis.
6. **Object to processing** or request restriction — email privacy@componi.dev.
7. **Lodge a complaint** with the Brazilian ANPD (<https://www.gov.br/anpd>) or your local supervisory authority.

We respond to rights requests within **15 days** (LGPD Art. 19).

## 7. Security

- All traffic is TLS-encrypted.
- Passwords are never stored — authentication is federated through GitHub/Google OAuth.
- Role-based access control, rate limiting, and append-only audit logs for privileged actions (see `docs/SECURITY.md`).
- We report qualifying incidents to the ANPD and to affected users within 72 hours of becoming aware (LGPD Art. 48).

## 8. Children

Componi is not directed at children under 16. If you believe a child has registered, email privacy@componi.dev and we will close the account.

## 9. International transfers

Supabase may store data in the EU or US. Transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (GDPR Art. 46) and, for LGPD, on the *adequação* or *cláusulas específicas* framework (LGPD Art. 33).

## 10. Changes

We update this policy by bumping the version string at the top. Material changes trigger a re-consent prompt on your next login (the `privacyAcceptedVersion` column tracks which version you agreed to). Non-material changes (typos, clarifications) do not.

Last version history is tracked in this file's Git history.
